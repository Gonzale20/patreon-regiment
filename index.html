How Operation Iron Tide Started

Iron Tide begins with silence. A Regiment convoy carrying fuel, repair supplies, and radio equipment disappears on Everon. What first appears to be a routine loss of contact quickly becomes proof of a larger enemy buildup.

The Convoy That Never Checked In

The last transmission is broken and incomplete: engine noise, distant armor fire, and a warning that heavy tracks were moving north. After that, the convoy goes dark.

The Enemy Line

Recon finds fortified checkpoints, sandbagged roads, concealed armor, and field command positions. The enemy is no longer passing through Everon. They are digging in.

The Mission

The Regiment is not being sent to patrol. It is being sent to break a hardened line, recover the truth behind the missing convoy, and remove the enemy’s ability to hold the island corridor. Armor opens the path. Infantry secures it. Logistics keeps the tide moving.

Reserved for Patreon

Patreon members receive priority consideration for the most important armored crew positions in Operation Iron Tide. This is not an automatic assignment board and it is not a command staffing tool. Final assignments are confirmed through the Regiment Discord before the operation.

Patreon focus: Tank Commander, Tank Gunner, and Tank Driver positions are the main reserved seats for supporters.
Patreon Priority

Tank Commander

Leads the vehicle crew, controls movement, calls targets, maintains spacing, and coordinates with squad leadership. This seat requires calm comms and good battlefield awareness.

Patreon Priority

Tank Gunner

Engages armor, fortified positions, and high-priority threats as directed by the commander. This role requires patience, target discipline, and clean confirmation before firing.

Patreon Priority

Tank Driver

Keeps the tank alive through movement, spacing, cover, and repositioning. A good driver protects the crew by avoiding overextension and reacting quickly to commander direction.

Infantry Roles for Non-Patreon Members

Non-Patreon members still have a major role in the operation. Infantry protects armor, clears close threats, secures objectives, and keeps the push from collapsing.

How Assignments Work

  • Patreon members get priority consideration for armored crew seats.
  • Command confirms final assignments before mission start.
  • Priority does not override reliability, communication, or mission needs.

Operation Standard

  • Armor does not push without infantry support.
  • Infantry does not outrun the armored column.
  • Comms stay short, useful, and mission-focused.
  • Everyone moves as one Regiment.
